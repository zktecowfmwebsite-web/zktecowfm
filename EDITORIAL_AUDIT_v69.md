# ZKTeco WFM Insights Editorial Pass — v69

## What changed

- All 55 Insights articles were reviewed against the locked editorial standard.
- The four articles already finalized with leadership were preserved as the benchmark, with only platform-neutral cleanup where appropriate.
- The other 51 articles received topic-specific ZKTeco WFM perspectives, takeaways and CTAs.
- 51 articles also received a short, concrete field/operational note to make the writing feel authored from real product and implementation experience rather than generated from a common template.
- Repeated generic decision language was removed.
- General articles are platform-neutral; Workday is used intentionally only in Workday-specific articles, with one exception in the middleware article where CirrusDCS must be distinguished from CirrusConnect.
- CirrusDCS is described as Workday-specific; CirrusConnect is described for software-partner device connectivity/operations.
- Remote diagnostic language is qualified by product/configuration and does not present roadmap-only screen-control capability as universal.
- Compliance-sensitive articles preserve customer policy/legal ownership and the standard educational disclaimer.

## Competitive/editorial pressure test

Public industry research was used to pressure-test the library against common competitor themes: purpose-built vs. tablet lifecycle, middleware, offline operation, biometrics/privacy, employee self-service, remote diagnostics, enterprise scalability and Workday marketplace positioning. The rewrite intentionally avoids copying competitor language and instead emphasizes ZKTeco WFM's own strengths: workforce-edge specialization, flexible architecture, Ultima hardware, TimeTrack, SDK/API choices, CirrusConnect for software partners, CirrusDCS for Workday, manufacturing/lifecycle depth, implementation and support.

## Locked writing standard

Each article must teach a practical decision, include tradeoffs, explain when a feature may not be appropriate, connect only to the ZKTeco products/services that genuinely matter, and end with a CTA specific to that article. Generic phrases and repeated sales copy are not acceptable.
