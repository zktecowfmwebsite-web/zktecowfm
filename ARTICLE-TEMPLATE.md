# ZKTeco WFM Insights — Locked Editorial Template v2

## Editorial purpose
Every article should teach the market how to evaluate workforce data collection, time-clock technology or workforce-edge decisions more intelligently. The article should be useful even if the reader never buys from ZKTeco WFM. ZKTeco's perspective should then show why our architecture, flexibility, products, manufacturing and services are relevant to that decision.

## Audience rules
- General articles: write for both enterprise customers and HCM/WFM/T&A software partners. Do not force Workday into the article.
- Software-partner articles: focus on platform extension, build-vs-buy, Android/SDK/app/cloud/API options, device operations, manufacturing and lifecycle.
- Workday-specific articles: explicitly address Workday customers and may reference Ultima, TimeTrack, CirrusDCS, certified integration, implementation and support.

## Standard structure
1. Hero: strong title + one-sentence thesis.
2. Opening insight: why this matters to the business.
3. Core argument: normally 3–5 concise sections.
4. Options / decision guidance where useful.
5. Questions leaders should ask: unique to the subject.
6. ZKTeco WFM Perspective: concise, factual, differentiated and tied to real products/services.
7. Key takeaway: one memorable conclusion.
8. Subject-specific CTA.
9. Educational disclaimer.

## Content rules
- Typical target: 800–1,100 words; go longer only when the subject genuinely requires it.
- Prefer 1–4 sentence paragraphs and concise lists.
- Explain why, when, tradeoffs and operating consequences.
- Do not repeat generic boilerplate across articles.
- Do not use unsupported superlatives, confidential counts, pricing or roadmap as current capability.
- Policy/legal/compliance decisions belong to the customer and its advisors; technology can support configured requirements.
- ZKTeco WFM strength should be connected to the topic through the relevant combination of Ultima, TimeTrack, CirrusConnect, CirrusDCS (Workday only), Android/SDK/API options, device/cloud technology, implementation/support and manufacturing/lifecycle.

## Locked visual system
All articles use `assets/insight-enhancements.css`. Article files must contain no inline global navigation, footer or typography CSS.
- Blog H1: max ~50px desktop.
- Subtitle/tagline: 17px desktop.
- Body: ~16.25px / 1.56 line-height.
- H2: ~27px.
- Compact paragraph/section spacing.
- Right rail: 300px; links 14px; supporting copy 13px.
- Shared boxes, questions, perspective, takeaway, disclaimer and CTA styles.

## Infrastructure rule
Do not alter global navigation/header/footer while editing an article. Global menu behavior lives only in `assets/site-enhancements.css`; Insights presentation lives only in `assets/insight-enhancements.css`.


## Human editorial standard (required)

Every article must sound like it was written by an experienced ZKTeco WFM product, implementation or executive leader—not generated from a content template. The structure may be consistent; the argument must not be.

- Open with a concrete operational observation, buying tension or field lesson specific to the topic.
- Do not reuse generic ZKTeco Perspective paragraphs or CTA headlines across articles.
- Connect the subject to the ZKTeco WFM capability that actually matters: Ultima hardware, TimeTrack, Platform SDK, CirrusConnect, CirrusDCS for Workday, APIs/integration, device operations, manufacturing/lifecycle, implementation or support. Do not force every product into every article.
- General articles should be platform-neutral. Mention Workday only when it genuinely improves the example; Workday-specific articles should be clearly written for Workday customers.
- Explain tradeoffs and when not to use a feature. Strong thought leadership is not a feature list.
- Prefer specific operating language over generic phrases such as digital transformation, seamless, best-in-class or future-proof.
- Make CTAs topic-specific and useful. The CTA should invite the reader to discuss the exact decision covered by the article.
- Use internal domain material as subject-matter input only; never copy confidential wording, roadmap statements or unsupported claims into public content.


## v77 Enterprise Thought-Leadership Depth Standard

For buyer-oriented Enterprise Time Clock articles, retain the standard editorial flow but require substantive decision support:

- At least one realistic operating scenario or deployment example.
- At least one “What most buyers overlook” insight that exposes a non-obvious operating or lifecycle issue.
- Failure modes or tradeoffs, not only feature descriptions.
- Concrete “What to ask” questions that distinguish shallow feature support from a credible operating model.
- A practical decision rule that tells the reader how to make the choice.
- ZKTeco WFM Perspective comes after the independent buyer reasoning; it should connect the reasoning to relevant capabilities without turning the article into a product brochure.
- Use enough detail that an enterprise buyer learns something new, but avoid implementation-manual detail and confidential internal mechanics.
- Visual treatment may vary by subject, while typography, spacing, sidebar, disclaimer and editorial hierarchy remain consistent.
