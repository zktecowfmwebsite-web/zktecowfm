COOKIE POLICY

Your Cookie & Privacy Choices.

Learn about the categories of cookies and similar technologies that may be used on the ZKTeco WFM website and how to manage your preferences.

Consent design: Necessary technologies are always available. Optional analytics and marketing categories are designed to remain off until selected by the visitor where consent is required. Production counsel and the web team should verify the actual technologies deployed before launch.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of information used by websites to support functionality, remember preferences, understand usage or support marketing activities.

Cookie Categories

Necessary

Required for core website functions and to remember privacy preferences. These cannot be disabled through the website preference center.

Analytics

May be used to understand aggregate website usage and improve content when analytics services are enabled. Where consent is required, these should not run until the visitor opts in.

Marketing

May support campaign measurement, advertising or related technologies when enabled. Where consent is required, these should not run until the visitor opts in.

Your Choices

You can accept all optional categories, reject non-essential categories, or choose categories individually. You may change your choice at any time using the Cookie Preferences link in the footer.

Third-Party Technologies

Third-party services may have their own privacy and cookie practices. The production cookie inventory should identify active providers, purposes and retention periods before launch.

Updates

This policy should be updated whenever the website adds or removes analytics, advertising, embedded media, chat, CRM tracking, forms or similar technologies.
