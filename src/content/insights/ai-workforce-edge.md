AI is only as useful as the workforce data it receives. Accurate capture, appropriate validation and reliable delivery create the trusted foundation AI needs to analyze, recommend and act intelligently.

## Start with trustworthy source data

Reliable workforce systems depend on clear time events, identity controls and resilient delivery to the systems that use the data.

## Use AI where it improves the workflow

Use intelligent assistance to identify exceptions, surface trends and support employees and managers—while retaining appropriate human review and governance.
